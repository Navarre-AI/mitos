# Use Case Ideas

Who would use Mitos, and for what. Add new ones at the bottom of a section
with the date. "Today" means it works now; the rest needs building (see
[Feature Ideas](Feature%20Ideas.md)).

## Today, inside FileMaker

1. **One search field for the whole solution.** A name, a phone tail, an
   email or a date finds the right record in whatever table it lives in.
   Front desk, phone support, sales. (2026-11-23)
2. **Caller lookup.** The last four digits of a number, a misspelled
   surname, or "Last, First". (2026-11-23)
3. **Names that aren't spelled the way you typed them.** Bob finds Robert,
   typos land. CRMs, member lists,
   patient and student rosters. (2026-11-23)
4. **Ranges without a find request.** "orders over 500", "March 2025".
   (2026-11-23)
5. **Search from a script.** `Insert from URL` to `/api/search` hands any
   script ranked JSON. Duplicate checks before a new record, lookups that
   pick a match. (2026-11-23)
6. **Search on FileMaker Go,** quick over a slow connection, because the
   work happens on the server. (2026-11-23)
7. **Search that stays up** while FileMaker is down or busy with a backup.
   (2026-11-23)
8. **Finding out what people look for and can't find.** The failed-searches
   report on the Log tab. (2026-11-23)
9. **Land on a search screen from anywhere.** A button or trigger goes to
   the Mitos layout with a search already run, and the person carries on
   from there: clicks a result, types another search. Works in Pro and Go;
   not in WebDirect yet. (2026-11-24)

## Today, with the AI stages on

10. **Search by meaning.** "the guy from the boat company", "that complaint
    about late delivery". (2026-11-23)
11. **Records that read badly** (product codes, terse notes) made findable
    through search notes. (2026-11-23)

## Standalone search sites (needs building)

A Mitos site on its own, outside FileMaker, for any subset of data and any
set of users. Matt, 2026-11-23: "A user can make an app with any subset of
data, or any set of users, for any type of search and deploy it as a
website separate from filemaker."

12. **A search site for one team,** one subset of data, no FileMaker seat
    each. Field staff, warehouse, reception. (2026-11-23)
13. **A customer-facing lookup.** Product catalogue, parts finder, member
    directory, order status. (2026-11-23)
14. **A partner or contractor portal** that shows only their slice.
    (2026-11-23)
15. **Search for an archive or legacy file** nobody wants to open in
    FileMaker any more. (2026-11-23)

## Further out

16. **One search across several FileMaker files or servers,** after a
    merger or for a split solution. (2026-11-23)
17. **Search for agents.** An AI assistant uses Mitos as its lookup tool
    instead of guessing at FileMaker finds. (2026-11-23)
